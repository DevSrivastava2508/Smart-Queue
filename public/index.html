Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Is SmartQueue free to use for patients? ▼

Yes! Generating a digital queue token, tracking live OPD progress, and receiving email confirmations is 100% free for all patients. Standard hospital consultation charges apply at hospital billing counters as usual.

How will I know when it is my turn? ▼

You can monitor your live queue token in real-time on our web tracker. We recommend arriving 10-15 minutes before your estimated time slot.

Can I print a physical ticket receipt? ▼

Yes. On the confirmation screen, SmartQueue includes a realistic thermal receipt printer simulator that you can print, save as PDF, or display on your mobile screen for hospital receptionist scanning.

What should I do in a severe medical emergency? ▼

If you or a patient experience acute chest pain, breathlessness, heavy bleeding, or loss of consciousness, do NOT wait for an OPD queue token. Immediately dial 108 / 112 or proceed directly to the nearest hospital Emergency Room.

How can I reach customer support? ▼

For any assistance with appointments, tokens, hospital timings, or technical queries, contact our 24/7 customer support team at smartqueue70@gmail.com.
